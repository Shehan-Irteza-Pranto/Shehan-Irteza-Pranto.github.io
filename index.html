About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Biomedical Informatics and Data Science at UAB (candidacy June 2026, expected graduation May 2028). My thesis, Continuous, Interpretable, and Personalized Deep Learning for Mortality Prediction in Critically Ill AKI Patients on CRRT, is supported by the NIH/NIDDK R01 Artificial Intelligence to Predict Outcomes in Patients with AKI on CRRT.

Day to day I design transformer, multi-instance and reinforcement-learning models over CRRT circuit-pressure waveforms and EHR data, and I am building a multi-agent LLM system that turns model outputs into clinician-readable explanations, with a governance harness that flags unreliable predictions for human review. I work closely with nephrologists and intensivists so that what we build fits bedside decision making.

I hold an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from The University of Alabama, where I built firmware, PCBs and software for the FRIENDS wearable vaping monitor in Prof. Edward Sazonov's CLAWS Lab, and a B.Sc. in EEE from Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology. As a research engineer in Prof. Khondaker A. Mamun's AIMS Lab (UIU, Dhaka) I built Bangla speech, speaker and face recognition modules for a hospital receptionist robot and smartphone screening tools for Parkinson's disease.

Looking ahead: I am interested in research and industry roles at the intersection of clinical AI, agentic systems and physiological time series. Get in touch: prantos@uab.edu

AI decision support from CRRT machine data

Minute-level circuit pressures from the bedside CRRT machine, plus the EHR, turned into rolling, interpretable predictions of mortality and RRT-free survival for critically ill patients with AKI. My PhD thesis project.
